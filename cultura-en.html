PASSION

In Brazil, football is part of everyday life and is connected to identity, emotions and the celebrations of its communities.

Beyond professional stadiums, this passion can also be experienced in neighborhoods, families and spaces where people gather to share their love for the sport.

CELEBRATION

Football is also present in the celebrations and gatherings of communities. Matches can become spaces where people share emotions and strengthen their relationships.

The joy of the fans is part of the cultural experience surrounding Brazilian football.

NEIGHBORHOOD FOOTBALL

Neighborhood football fields are spaces where children, young people and adults can come together around football.

These spaces allow sport to become part of community life and the everyday experiences of different groups.

BRAZIL IN THE WORLD

Brazilian football has gained international recognition and has helped project different aspects of Brazilian culture.

The history of its teams, players and fans has allowed Brazilian football to be recognized both inside and outside the country.
